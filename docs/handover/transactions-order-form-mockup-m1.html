Design notes — state after the 2026-08-28/29 review round

  1. Decided — settlement confirmation. With exactly one match the position is pre-filled but "Book now" stays inactive until the confirm box is ticked. One deliberate click on every order; no silent default (D-1).
  2. Decided — settlement date. Optional, visible, recorded only; the hint says so. Valuta-accurate booking stays a named successor.
  3. Decided — ticket persistence. The draft row (and with it the ticket number, case_number precedent) is created on the first explicit gesture — Save as draft, Propose, or Book now — never on opening the composer. The header reads "New ticket · Unsaved" until then; the chrome toggle above shows both header states. No orphaned drafts, no burnt numbers.
  4. Resolved pending final confirmation — OP-06. The edit-refusal clause is removed entirely: booking is never refused and CRUD edits are never refused, whatever the cash balance. The negative-balance warning carries the resulting balance and the flag notice, nothing more. Corrections stay possible through the CRUD (ADR-0128 §6 depends on that path). Named successors recorded in M-4: a Watch Desk watchpoint on negative cash; portfolio-level external contributions / withdrawals as their own flow, outside the trade-ticket object.
  5. Impact preview. S6 will need room. The natural slot is between Amounts and Settlement, or as a right-hand rail. Nothing is reserved here yet.
  6. Flow chooser. Five entry points, with direction living inside the order form rather than as two separate cards. Alternative: six cards with Buy and Sell split apart.
  7. Currency mismatch cannot be reached from this form — currency is derived from the investment and shown read-only in the context strip. The block from the validation matrix stays a service-layer guard with no surface here.

Design notes — decisions this mockup proposes

  1. Compact single-page forms, not wizards. The reported flows have fewer moving parts than U-NEW; each fits one page with the same block order as M-1 (what happened → amounts → on booking → settlement → messages → actions). Master data lives on the ticket as payload; the investment row is an emission effect at booking (M-2 decision applied to R-COMMIT and R-SEC-BUY).
  2. "On booking" is the teaching block. Every effect the emission will write is a visible row — flow (distribution · actual), NAV, status, commitment, cash leg — before the user books. Nothing the booking does is a surprise afterwards.
  3. R-SEC-SELL consequences are emission rows, not options. NAV → 0 and "investment set inactive" are inherent to a full sale, so they appear as rows in the On-booking block rather than as checkboxes. The optional set-inactive checkbox exists only in U-SELL, where selling all units is a choice, not the flow's definition.
  4. R-2 refusal. "Sell part of the stake" is selectable, and selecting it produces the red refusal explaining why (NAV and unfunded must restate proportionally; interacts with plan flows) and naming the successor. Book, Propose and Save as draft are all disabled while it is selected — a partial-sale ticket cannot even exist as a draft in v1, so nothing half-designed lingers in the blotter.
  5. R-COMMIT has no settlement block at all. Its absence is explained by the blue panel ("no cash moves with this ticket"), not silent. Capital calls stay ordinary cashflows outside the ticket; the ticket is the provenance anchor.
  6. Discount/premium surfaced, never judged. R-SEC-BUY derives price vs. acquired NAV, R-SEC-SELL derives proceeds vs. last reported NAV — as neutral info rows in the Amounts block. Secondary discounts are normal economics, so they are context, not warnings.
  7. AnlV gate. Applies to R-COMMIT and R-SEC-BUY (both create investments) on Propose and Book now, exactly as in M-2; the draft may dangle. R-SEC-SELL touches an existing investment and has no gate.
  8. Negative cash on R-SEC-BUY warns with the resulting balance, exactly as in M-1 (toggle "cash short" above). Nothing blocks.

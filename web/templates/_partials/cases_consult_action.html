{# "Consult Shirley" (ADR-0138 §10, D-CS-B-110) — the case page's in-place hand-off, the Watch Desk's D-WD-23 idiom. Included in the file's actions row on an open or waiting case (cases_file.html); a closed case renders no actions (D-CS-B-76). The form posts to the chat surface (POST /chat/brief/case/{case_id}), which sets the session's consultation stash on the case and answers with this same form (a quiet swap in place) plus an HX-Trigger that opens Shirley's dock and refreshes its banner; a closed, unknown or foreign case re-renders without either. Nothing is written anywhere. The PM stays on the case. pf-case__action-form lets the form sit in the actions row as the Resume now form does; the button keeps the row's action hooks. Context: consult_shirley_post, csrf_token. #}

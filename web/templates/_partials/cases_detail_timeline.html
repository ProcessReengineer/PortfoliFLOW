{# Case timeline (ADR-0107, C3a) — the append-only spine, oldest first. Per-kind bodies: opened — "Case opened" with the origin phrasing. These two payload keys are the rendering contract; C3b's composer writes them. closed — "Case closed" with the closing note (payload["closing_note"], written by C1's close()). pin (document) — the pin anatomy (ADR-0107 §7): the curation comment plus the attached artifact — filename linking to the download endpoint and its human-legible size. Rendered verbatim from the payload, nothing recomputed. pin (consultation) — the curated Shirley excerpt (ADR-0107 C6): the PM's curation comment plus the quoted text, attributed to Shirley (the words are Shirley's; the actor chip stays ``pm``, the curator — binding decision 3). Read verbatim from the payload. pin (chart_snapshot) — the frozen Shirley chart (ADR-0114): the curation comment plus the caption and the figure itself, re-plotted from the stored spec through the shared render helper (``chart_snapshot.js``). Any other kind or artifact — a seeded row of an unrendered kind — falls through to the calm generic fallback: the head row alone (kind label + actor + timestamp), payload untouched, so it never breaks the page. Closed cases render identically — reading is never gated (C3b's composers are gated on the open state, but they live in the rail, not here).

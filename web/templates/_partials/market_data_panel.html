{# Market-data live-import schedule panel (ADR-0093). Swapped into #pf-market-data-panel by both the save form (POST /api/market-data/schedule) and the refresh-now form (POST /api/market-data/refresh-now). Both inherit the container's hx-target="this" / hx-swap="innerHTML". Context (via web.routes.market_data._panel_context): csrf_token, cadence_choices, cadence_labels, hours, current{cadence,preferred_hour,timezone,enabled,next_due_at,last_run_at, next_due_display,last_run_display,configured}, schedule_saved, schedule_error, refresh_message, poll_since (encoded ISO 8601 — set only by a refresh-now confirmation). The two status stamps arrive pre-formatted as ``*_display`` strings, already in the schedule's own timezone. Formatting them here instead would print whatever zone the stored datetime carries — UTC — so an owner who anchors the schedule in Europe/Berlin would read a due time an hour or two off the hour they had just chosen. #}

Live import augments listed instruments (with a primary ISIN / ticker / FIGI) from external providers. It never overwrites Excel data. The refresh itself runs in the scheduler (ADR-0117); this panel sets the interval, can queue an out-of-cadence run, and shows when the last one landed.

{# The negative-cash indicator (A-11, ADR-0130, S5 P-5c). One line per cash position standing below zero today, each linking to its detail page. Never a sum across currencies: a EUR overdraft and a USD one are two facts, not one. The shape is the record's own (mock01 `notice()`, P-UX-A1e2): a `pf-note--warn` whose lead counts the positions, whose figures are `pf-neg`, and whose two standing sentences sit behind a "Why this is flagged" disclosure — the third placement of the A-18 sentence, after the Blotter's (A1a) and the impact panel's. The record draws the one-position case with its detail inline in the lead (`pf-note__inline`); several positions cannot all ride there, so they stack as `pf-note__sub` lines instead. `investments/detail.html` draws the same block for its one position, which is what makes this one indicator rather than two. No button, no dismiss, no gesture. The indicator is informational on every surface and blocks nothing (ADR-0130), and there is nothing to acknowledge because there is no flag to clear — the state is re-derived on every render (InvestmentService.list_negative_cash, T-5 D-Z), so it clears itself the moment the ledger comes back to zero or above. **This file owns the wrapper's attributes, and is the only file that writes them.** The element re-fetches itself, so the shell rendered by the no-DB Area render and the populated response are the same element at two moments of its life; a shell whose trigger string had drifted would simply stop refreshing. _negative_cash_shell.html therefore includes *this* template with an empty count rather than restating the markup. Which is also why "no negative cash" is an **empty wrapper** rather than no wrapper: the element is the thing that asks. The wrapper carries no class since P-UX-A1e2 — it is furniture, not a component — so the `:empty` rule that collapses it to nothing visible rides on its id, scoped to the Area, the way the mono holdouts do. The `{% if %}` opens flush against the `>` and closes flush against the `` for that rule's sake: `:empty` counts a whitespace text node as content, so an indented body would have left the rule matching nothing and the empty wrapper holding its margin open. The refresh contract (T-5 D-X): `load` covers the first render; `htmx:afterRequest` from the composer host and from the History section cover the only two gestures that can change a cash balance — a booking completes inside #tx-composer-host, a reversal inside #history. No polling, and neither the composer nor the history route knows this element exists: the indicator listens, it is not told. Why #history and not #tx-history (T-5 D-AA): htmx resolves a `from:` selector when it registers the trigger, not on each event, and History is lazy — #tx-history does not exist yet when this element is processed. #history is the Area section: present in the shell, and never swapped.

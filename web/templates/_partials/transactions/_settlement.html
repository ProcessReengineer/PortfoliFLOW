{# The MD-3 settlement panel — one renderer for every composer in this Area. Extracted from `_order_derived.html` when the R-SEC-SELL composer (S4c) acquired a settlement block of its own. It is the same question on every flow that moves cash — "which cash position did the money move on, and are you sure" — answered from the same four context keys, so a second copy of this four-state chain is how two surfaces would come to offer creation in one state and refuse it in another. The four states are MD-3's three plus operator decision D-F: no currency yet, nothing in the currency (offer to create), rows exist but every one is retired (do not offer), and candidates to pick from. Which one applies is decided in the route and handed here as exclusive booleans — no rule is restated in Jinja. Copy is MD-9's, lifted verbatim from M-1; M-3 states that its settlement blocks "behave exactly as in M-1", and the way to keep that true is for them to be the same template. Context: currency, candidates, offer_cash_creation, inactive_cash_only, settle_confirmed, entered, currency_from_form. #}

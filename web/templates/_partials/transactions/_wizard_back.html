{# The wizard's Back control. A **GET**, and that is the whole design: Back writes nothing (D-AH). It re-renders the previous step from the draft the ticket already is, rather than saving the current step on the way out — going backwards is how an operator inspects what they entered, and a step that saved itself on the way back would let a half-corrected field overwrite a good one. The consequence is worth stating plainly, because it is the price: an edit made on this step and not confirmed with Continue is not carried backwards. What comes back is what the ticket holds. The step is a query parameter rather than stored state; see `_resume_step` for what the same endpoint answers when no step is named. Context: step, ticket_id. #}

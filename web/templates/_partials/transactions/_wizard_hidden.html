{# The wizard's field carry — every value the active step does not show. Each Continue posts the *whole* master-data inventory, because the draft's `master_data` is written as a full replacement rather than merged (`_ComposerForm.master_data`). A field that only existed on the step it was typed on would therefore be erased by the next Continue — so the fields the step does not render ride here instead. `visible` names what the step draws; everything else in the inventory is emitted as a hidden input. Stating it as an exclusion rather than as a per-step list of hidden fields is deliberate: a field added to the inventory carries automatically, and only a step that means to *show* it has to say so. Empty values are still emitted. A missing input and an empty one mean the same thing to `master_data()` (both omit the key), and emitting all of them keeps this template free of a second rule about which absences matter.

{# Calibration section body (ADR-0089; renamed from Watchlist per DC4/D5; grown into the tenant calibration editor per ADR-0116 §7). Three panels, in this order: the calibration editor (what the beat is calibrated to), the watchpoint list (what it watches), and the cadence panel (when it runs). The three read-only `calib-cell` facts that used to sit on top are superseded by the editor — they stated three of the values it now renders editable, and keeping them would be a second, staler view of the same numbers. Each panel posts to its own endpoint and swaps its own target, so none of them can clobber another. The cadence caption states only the instruction. Its earlier wording also carried the ADR-0086 rationale — that cadence is a domain calibration interface for the heartbeat, not a deployment artifact — which is background the operator does not need on screen while setting one; it is kept in ``save_cadence``'s docstring and in the ADR, so do not restore it here as a missing rationale. Context: the editor context (see _calibration_context), the watchpoint list context (see _watchpoint_list_context) and the cadence-panel context (see _cadence_context). #}

{# "Watch list" — the live monitor beneath the Briefing findings (ADR-0089, DC2; edit affordances per ADR-0116 §6; anatomy per M-WD-1 v2 and P-UX-A6-a2). Two honesty rules are load-bearing here and must not be "tidied" away: * Every meter runs 0 → ceiling with the WARN mark at that subject's WARN fraction of the ceiling. The *scale* is identical on every row — the scenario-comparison axis rule applied to utilisation — and the mark is never moved to flatter a row. Since ADR-0116 §3 the WARN fraction is per subject (a watchpoint overlay may override the tenant default), so the mark is per-subject **positioned** from `row.warn_threshold_pct`; it is still never *rescaled*, and the row it marks is classified against exactly that threshold. Never rescale per row; never move the mark away from the threshold in force. A breach clamps the rendered fill to 100% while the printed figures stay honest. * Irene's note is assembled in the route from live status + the subject's acknowledged watch-state + the tenant's effective Floor Config. The template renders the assembled string and nothing more — it never composes a claim of its own. The head makes no observation-time claim at all, so a tenant whose beat has never run reads no bogus "last seen" stamp. A third rule joins them with the mute affordance: a muted row stays **visible**, tagged, and counted in its group row, and its status keeps rendering live. Mute suppresses finding creation, never truth — so this template must never hide a row, grey out a figure, or drop a muted subject from a count. A group renders **closed** on first load. Its group row is the decision surface — name, state, muted count, subject count and the effective set — so it already carries what decides whether opening the slot is worth it, and the calm-by-default posture the Watch Desk keeps toward the book applies to its own first screen too. Closed is not hidden: every row stays in the document, the group counts stay live, and a muted row is still visible, tagged and counted one click away. The honesty rules above are unaffected — this changes disclosure, not content. And from the signal fragment, for the families that can fail to observe: a **no-data** row draws no meter at all; signal families say Calm / Approaching / Triggered and never "breach"; liquidity shows ratios, never its internal 100-scale; the freshness calm remainder is collapsed, never omitted, and counted. Anatomy (D-UX-A6-3): one `pf-table`. Every family is a group row and, beneath it, a slot row holding the family's sub-rows in a nested `pf-table`. The slot is server-rendered with the page and closed by `hidden` (D-A6-41, Q-UX-S-A6-10): there is no per-family fragment route, and every row must stay in the document. The chevron toggles it in place. Subject keys leave the visible cells and live in each group's "Subject keys" disclosure (ADR-0133 §4, R12). Context: monitor — coverage_available, groups, rss (None while Press coverage is off), signal_groups, signal_footer; csrf_token. #}

Every family, one row per live identity: the four defined families whose watchpoint *is* the subject, and the three derived ones whose watchpoint is a sensitivity overlay over a subject the limit sets or the curated tag vocabulary already name. Both belong here — "what does this tenant watch, and how nervously" is one question, and answering half of it somewhere else is how the two halves drift. Read-only apart from the three row actions. Adjust reuses the drawer the watch list opens (one editor, two surfaces); History renders the identity's version rows; both land in the row's slot. Retire… opens the R7 confirmation in a hidden slot row beneath (D-A6-70) — no native confirm — and its Retire writes a retiring version, after which the row moves to the collapsed list below. There is deliberately **no add affordance here**. Adding a watchpoint happens on the watch list beneath the Briefing, beside the live rows it will join, because the question "should I watch this" is answered by looking at what is already being watched — not by looking at a settings table. The Sensitivity column (ADR-0133 §3.3) states the family's preset for a row without an override and Custom for a row with one; Press rows have none. The subject cell carries the display name; the keys live in the "Subject keys" disclosure beneath the table (the a2 idiom).

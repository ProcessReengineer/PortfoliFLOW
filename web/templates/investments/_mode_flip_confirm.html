This dialog states the three consequences the operator cannot discover afterwards: the irreversibility, the live-row deletion (and what it does *not* touch), and the immediate materialisation. English only, per ADR-0008. #}

Switch to unitised valuation?

NAV for this investment will be computed from its transaction ledger and per-unit price series (holdings × price) instead of being carried as reported statement values.

  • This change is one-way. It cannot be undone from this surface; corrections go through ledger edits.
  • NAV rows written by the live market-data ingest will be deleted. Rows imported from Excel or entered manually are never touched.
  • Computed NAV rows are written immediately for every date that has both a price and a positive holding.
